## Context

CapeScape is the group's AI-assisted, accessible tourism app for Cape Town. A solo design
session already settled the interaction model (a conversational assistant, voice + text, as
the *primary* way users interact — not a chat widget bolted onto a browse app), the platform
plan (mobile first, smart glasses as a phase-2 wearable), and a consent model for
personalization data. What's still open needs the team's judgment, not more solo research.
