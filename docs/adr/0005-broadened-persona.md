# Primary persona: non-negotiable trip requirements, not one disability category

An earlier research pass surfaced strong citations for a narrower persona — visually
impaired and mobility-impaired tourists specifically. After team discussion, the team
decided the primary persona is broader: travelers whose visual or mobility disability,
dietary/health needs (e.g. allergies), or group composition (e.g. traveling as a family)
impose Constraints an Itinerary must satisfy. This folds the earlier citation-backed
narrow persona into a wider group, and means Constraints (accessibility, dietary,
group-size) are modeled uniformly rather than treating disability as a special case with
its own rules.

_See ADR-0007: hearing disability was intended to be part of this broadened persona but was
dropped from the wording above — corrected there._
